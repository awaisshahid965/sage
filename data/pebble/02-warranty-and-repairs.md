# Warranty and Repairs Policy

This policy covers faults that develop after purchase. Faults reported within
30 days of delivery are usually better handled as a return under POL-RET-001,
because a return gives the customer their money back while this policy gives
repair or replacement.

## 1. Warranty periods

Warranty length depends on the **component**, not on the order. A single
product can therefore carry two different periods at once.

| Item type | Warranty from delivery |
| --- | --- |
| Devices (headphones, tablets, speakers, wearables) | **24 months** |
| **Rechargeable batteries and cells** | **12 months** |
| Charging cables, cases, straps, and other accessories | **6 months** |
| Replacement parts fitted by Pebble | 12 months, or the remainder of the original, whichever is longer |
| Refurbished devices sold as "Pebble Renewed" | 12 months |

This is the most misread table in the corpus. A Pebble Drift ANC bought on
1 March 2025 has device cover until 1 March 2027, but its internal battery is
covered only until 1 March 2026. A customer reporting poor battery life at
month 18 is outside battery cover while still inside device cover.

Battery capacity is considered faulty only when it falls **below 70% of rated
capacity** under Pebble diagnostic test. Gradual decline above that threshold
is classed as normal wear and is not a fault.

## 2. What is covered

- manufacturing defects in materials or workmanship
- component failure under normal described use
- firmware faults that cannot be resolved by update or reset
- audio driver failure, including single-sided dropout on headphones
- charging port failure not caused by impact or liquid

## 3. What is not covered

- liquid ingress, including sweat damage on products not rated IPX4 or higher
- cracked screens, dents, scuffs, and other cosmetic damage
- damage from third-party chargers exceeding the rated input
- repairs or opening attempted by anyone other than Pebble or an authorised
  partner. This voids all remaining cover immediately
- loss and theft
- normal wear: ear tip degradation, strap discolouration, cable fraying at the
  strain relief
- consumables: ear tips, foam pads, screen protectors
- devices with a removed, altered, or illegible serial number

## 4. PebbleCare+

PebbleCare+ is an optional extended plan, purchasable within **60 days of
delivery** and not afterwards.

| Plan | Price | Adds |
| --- | --- | --- |
| PebbleCare+ 12 | £29 | 12 months on top of the standard device period |
| PebbleCare+ 24 | £49 | 24 months on top of the standard device period |
| PebbleCare+ Accident | £69 | 24 months, and covers one accidental damage claim per year with a £25 excess |

PebbleCare+ extends the **device** period. It does not extend the 12-month
battery period or the 6-month accessory period, which is a frequent complaint.
Only PebbleCare+ Accident covers liquid or impact damage.

Plans are tied to the device serial number and transfer with the device if it
is sold or gifted.

## 5. Making a claim

1. The customer raises a claim in the account area or with an agent, quoting
   the order reference in the format `PB-nnnn`.
2. Pebble runs a remote diagnostic where the device supports it.
3. If unresolved, Pebble issues a prepaid shipping label. The customer never
   pays to send a warranty item.
4. The device is assessed within **3 business days** of arrival.
5. Repair, replacement, or rejection is communicated by email.

Proof of purchase is required. A Pebble order reference is sufficient; a bank
statement alone is not. Devices bought from authorised resellers are covered on
production of the reseller's receipt.

## 6. Turnaround

| Stage | Target |
| --- | --- |
| Assessment after arrival | 3 business days |
| Standard repair | 7–10 business days |
| Repair requiring parts on backorder | Up to 21 business days |
| Replacement dispatch, in stock | 1 business day |

Where a repair is expected to exceed **10 business days** and the original
order value was **over £300**, the customer is offered a loan device for the
duration at no cost. Loan devices are current-generation but may be a different
colour. Failure to return a loan device within 14 days of the repair being
returned incurs a charge at full retail price.

## 7. Rejected claims

Where assessment finds the fault is not covered, the customer is offered:

- a chargeable repair quote, valid for 30 days
- return of the device unrepaired, free of charge
- recycling free of charge, with a 10% discount code toward a replacement

Pebble does not dispose of a device without explicit instruction. Devices left
unclaimed for 90 days after a rejected quote are recycled and the customer is
notified twice before this happens.

## 8. Right to repair and parts

Pebble publishes repair manuals and sells genuine parts for devices released
after January 2024. Self-repair using genuine Pebble parts does **not** void
the remaining warranty on unrelated components, but does void cover on the
component the customer worked on.

This is a deliberate narrowing of the clause in section 3. Where the two
appear to conflict, this section governs for devices released after
January 2024, and section 3 governs for everything older.

## 9. Escalation

Warranty rejections may be appealed once. Appeals are reviewed by the
Aftersales lead within 5 business days and the outcome is final internally.
Customers retain the right to pursue the matter through their statutory
consumer protections or through the retailer they purchased from.
