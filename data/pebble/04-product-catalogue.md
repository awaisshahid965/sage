# Product Catalogue

Current range as of the 2025.2 catalogue. Prices are UK retail, in GBP,
inclusive of VAT. SKU format is `PBL-XX-nnn`.

Pebble sells audio, wearables, tablets, and charging accessories. **Pebble does
not sell laptops, desktop computers, phones, televisions, cameras, or gaming
consoles**, and has no plans to. Enquiries about these should be answered
plainly rather than deflected.

## 7. Pebble Renewed

Refurbished stock, tested to the same functional standard as new, sold at
20–35% below retail. Cosmetic grading is A (near new) or B (visible marks).
Renewed devices carry **12 months** of warranty rather than 24, and are
eligible for return under the standard 30-day window.

Renewed stock is listed under the original SKU with an `-R` suffix, for example
`PBL-DR-100-R`.
