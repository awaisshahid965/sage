# Returns and Refunds Policy

This policy governs the return of goods purchased from Pebble Ltd through
pebble.example, the Pebble mobile app, or Pebble telephone sales. It does not
cover faults arising after the return window, which are handled under the
Warranty and Repairs Policy (POL-WAR-002).

## 1. Standard return window

Customers may return most items within **30 calendar days of the delivery
date**. The delivery date is the date recorded by the carrier as "delivered",
not the date of dispatch and not the date of order.

Returns requested on day 31 or later are refused automatically by the returns
portal. Agents cannot override this; see section 9 for the discretionary
process.

To be accepted, a returned item must be:

- in its original packaging, including inner trays and protective film
- complete with all supplied accessories, cables, and printed documentation
- free of engraving, personalisation, or user-applied labels
- deregistered from the customer's Pebble account, where applicable

## 2. Category exceptions

The 30-day window does **not** apply uniformly. The following categories carry
shorter windows or are excluded entirely.

| Category | Window | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sealed audio (headphones, earbuds) | 30 days | Full window applies while seal is intact |
| **Opened audio (headphones, earbuds)** | **14 days** | Hygiene restriction. Applies from delivery, not from opening |
| Wearables with skin contact (fitness bands, straps) | 14 days | As above |
| Digital licences and software keys | **No returns** | Excluded once the key is revealed in the account area |
| Gift cards and store credit | **No returns** | Non-refundable and non-transferable. See section 7 |
| Clearance and "Last Chance" items | 14 days | Marked at point of sale |
| Made-to-order and engraved items | **No returns** | Unless faulty |
| Bundles and multi-buy sets | 30 days | Must be returned complete; partial returns are refused |

The opened-audio restriction is the single most common source of disputes.
Opening the outer sleeve does not count as opening the product; breaking the
inner hygiene seal does.

## 3. Restocking fees

A restocking fee of **15% of the item price** applies where **all** of the
following are true:

1. the item has been opened, and
2. the item is not faulty, and
3. the item price is **over £200**

Items priced at £200 or below never attract a restocking fee. Faulty items
never attract a restocking fee, regardless of price. Items returned sealed
never attract a restocking fee.

Worked example: a Pebble Drift ANC (£249) returned opened and working incurs a
£37.35 fee, so the customer receives £211.65. A Pebble Drift Lite (£129)
returned opened and working incurs no fee.

## 4. Faulty goods

An item is treated as faulty where it fails to perform as described within the
return window. Faulty returns:

- carry no restocking fee
- include a prepaid return label in all territories, including international
- may be replaced rather than refunded, at the customer's choice
- extend to a full refund including original outbound shipping

Where a fault is reported after the 30-day return window, it is handled as a
warranty claim, not a return. This distinction matters: returns give money
back, warranty gives repair or replacement.

## 5. Return shipping costs

| Situation | Who pays outbound return |
| --- | --- |
| Item faulty, any territory | Pebble |
| Item not as described | Pebble |
| Change of mind, United Kingdom | Pebble (free returns label) |
| Change of mind, EU | Customer |
| Change of mind, rest of world | Customer |
| Wrong item shipped by Pebble | Pebble |

International customers returning by their own arrangement should declare the
parcel as "returned goods" to avoid import duty being levied on Pebble. Duty
incurred through incorrect declaration is deducted from the refund.

## 6. Refund timing

Refunds are processed within **5 business days** of the returned item being
received and inspected at the Pebble returns centre. Processing is the point at
which Pebble releases the money; it is not the point at which the customer sees
it.

Time for funds to appear, after processing:

| Method | Time to appear |
| --- | --- |
| Debit or credit card | 3–10 business days |
| PayPal | 1–3 business days |
| Apple Pay / Google Pay | 3–10 business days (follows the underlying card) |
| Klarna | Up to 14 days, and Klarna adjusts the instalment plan directly |
| Store credit | Immediate |

Customers frequently confuse the 5-day processing window with the 3–10 day card
window. The full worst case for a card refund is therefore 15 business days
from the parcel arriving.

## 7. Gift cards and store credit

Gift cards are non-refundable and cannot be returned, exchanged for cash, or
reissued if lost. This is an exception to every other clause in this policy.

Where an order was paid for partly by gift card and partly by another method,
refunds are apportioned: the gift card portion returns as store credit, the
remainder returns to the original payment method.

Store credit issued as a goodwill gesture expires after 12 months. Store credit
issued as a refund does not expire.

## 8. Exchanges

Pebble does not operate a direct exchange process. To swap an item for a
different model or colour, the customer returns the original for refund and
places a new order. Where stock is limited, agents may place a hold on the
replacement for 7 days pending receipt of the return.

## 9. Discretionary acceptance

Team leads may accept a return outside the stated window in the following
circumstances only:

- the delay is attributable to a documented Pebble error, including a delayed
  or misrouted delivery
- the customer is covered by consumer protection law granting a longer period
  in their territory
- the item was purchased as a gift within the last 60 days and is unopened
- a bereavement or hospitalisation is disclosed

Discretionary acceptances are logged against the agent's ID and reviewed
monthly. There is no discretion available on digital licences or gift cards.

## 10. Statutory rights

Nothing in this policy affects the customer's statutory rights. Customers in
the United Kingdom retain rights under the Consumer Rights Act 2015, and
customers in the European Union retain the 14-day right of withdrawal, which
runs in parallel to (and never shorter than) the windows above.
