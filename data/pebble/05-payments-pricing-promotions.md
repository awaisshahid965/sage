# Payments, Pricing and Promotions Policy

## 1. Accepted payment methods

| Method | Available | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Visa, Mastercard, American Express | All territories | Amex not accepted below £5 |
| PayPal | All territories | |
| Apple Pay, Google Pay | All territories | |
| Klarna, 3 instalments | UK and EU only | Orders £50–£1,000 |
| Klarna, pay in 30 days | UK only | Orders £50–£800 |
| Pebble gift card | All territories | Combinable with one other method |
| Bank transfer | Business accounts only | Orders over £2,000 |

Pebble does not accept cheques, cryptocurrency, cash on delivery, or more than
two payment methods on a single order.

## 2. When you are charged

Cards are **authorised** at checkout and **captured at dispatch**. The customer
sees a pending authorisation immediately and an actual charge when the parcel
leaves. For pre-orders and backorders the authorisation is re-taken every 7
days until dispatch, which some banks display as repeated pending charges.

Klarna is the exception: Klarna captures the full amount at checkout and
settles with the customer on its own schedule, so a Klarna order that is later
cancelled is refunded through Klarna rather than by Pebble.

## 3. Price match

Pebble matches a lower price on an identical item, subject to all of the
following:

1. the claim is made **within 14 days** of the Pebble purchase date
2. the competitor is a **UK-authorised retailer** of the brand
3. the item is identical: same SKU, same colour, same storage
4. the competitor has it **in stock** and available for immediate delivery
5. the competitor price is publicly listed and verifiable at time of claim

Not eligible for price match:

- marketplace sellers, including third-party sellers on large marketplaces
- auction sites and classified listings
- membership-only or warehouse-club pricing
- clearance, liquidation, ex-display, refurbished, or open-box stock
- prices resulting from a competitor's obvious pricing error
- bundle prices where the bundle contents differ
- Pebble's own past prices, including prices during a sale that has ended

The difference is refunded to the original payment method. Price match does not
apply to Pebble Renewed stock, and cannot be combined with a promotional code.

Note the asymmetry with returns: the price match window is **14 days** while
the standard return window is **30 days**. A customer on day 20 who finds a
better price cannot price match, but can still return and rebuy.

## 4. Promotional codes

- One code per order. Codes never stack with each other.
- Codes apply to product value only, never to shipping or gift cards.
- Codes cannot be applied retrospectively to a completed order.
- Where a code is applied and part of the order is later returned, the discount
  is apportioned across the returned items, so the refund is the price actually
  paid rather than the list price.
- Codes are void on Pebble Renewed and on clearance stock unless stated.

Standing discounts count as the one permitted code. A student cannot also use a

## 5. Sale periods and price protection

If Pebble reduces the price of an item **within 14 days** of a customer's
purchase, the difference is refunded on request. This is price protection and
is separate from price match in section 3, which concerns competitors.

Price protection does not apply during:

- Black Friday week, defined as the Monday before to the Monday after
- the January sale, 26 December to 7 January
- clearance events flagged as "final reductions"

The reason is that these are announced in advance and the reduction is the
event, not a price change.

## 6. Trade-in

Pebble accepts trade-in of eligible Pebble devices only. Third-party devices
are not accepted.

| Condition | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Working, cosmetically good | Up to 40% of current retail |
| Working, visible damage | Up to 20% |
| Not working | £10 flat, any device, as a recycling credit |

Trade-in value is issued as store credit, not cash, and is valid for 12 months.
The customer has 21 days to send the traded device after the quote; the quote
expires afterwards. Devices arriving in worse condition than declared are
requoted, and the customer may accept or have the device returned free.

## 7. Business and education

Business accounts receive 30-day payment terms after three successful orders,
volume pricing above 10 units, and consolidated invoicing. Education
institutions receive an additional 5% on tablets, which is the only case where
a Slate discount is permitted.

VAT-registered EU business customers may supply a VAT number for zero-rating.
This changes the price shown, and consequently the refund value, so returns on
zero-rated orders are refunded at the zero-rated amount.

## 8. Currency and pricing errors

Prices are charged in GBP, EUR, or USD depending on delivery territory, and the
displayed price is the price charged. Pebble does not add a currency
conversion fee; the customer's bank may.

Where an item is listed at an obviously incorrect price, Pebble reserves the
right to cancel the order and refund in full before dispatch. Pebble does not
cancel after dispatch for pricing errors.
