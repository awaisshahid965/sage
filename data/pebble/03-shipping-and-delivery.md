# Shipping and Delivery Policy

All times below are given in **business days**, meaning Monday to Friday
excluding UK public holidays. They are counted from dispatch, not from order.

## 1. Dispatch cut-off

Orders placed before **15:00 GMT** on a business day are dispatched the same
day. Orders placed after the cut-off, at weekends, or on a public holiday are
dispatched on the next business day.

The cut-off is 15:00 GMT year round. Pebble does not shift the cut-off for
British Summer Time, so between late March and late October the effective local
cut-off is 16:00 BST.

Orders containing a made-to-order or engraved item are dispatched as a single
parcel once the personalised item is ready, which adds 2–3 business days. A
customer wanting the rest sooner must place two orders.

## 2. United Kingdom

| Service | Time after dispatch | Cost | Free over |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Standard | 3–5 business days | £3.99 | £50 |
| Express | Next business day | £7.99 | £150 |
| Saturday delivery | Next Saturday | £11.99 | Never free |
| Click and collect | 2–4 business days | Free | — |

Express orders placed before the cut-off on a Thursday arrive Friday. Express
orders placed after the cut-off on a Thursday arrive Monday, because Express
does not run at weekends unless Saturday delivery is purchased.

Highlands and Islands, Northern Ireland, Isle of Man, and the Channel Islands
are served on Standard only, at 5–7 business days. Express is unavailable to
these postcodes regardless of what the checkout offers.

## 3. European Union

Pebble ships to all EU member states. **Germany, and therefore Berlin, is
served on the standard EU service.**

| Service | Time after dispatch | Cost | Free over |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| EU Standard | 5–8 business days | €9.99 | €150 |
| EU Express | 2–3 business days | €24.99 | Never free |

Since 2021 Pebble has operated as a registered EU importer. Prices shown to EU
customers at checkout are **inclusive of duty and local VAT**, so there is
nothing further to pay on delivery. Customers who are asked for a payment by
the carrier should refuse it and contact support, quoting the order reference.

## 4. Rest of world

| Region | Time after dispatch | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Switzerland and Norway | 5–9 business days | €14.99 |
| United States and Canada | 7–12 business days | $19.99 |
| Australia and New Zealand | 10–16 business days | $29.99 |
| Japan, Singapore, South Korea | 8–14 business days | $24.99 |
| Rest of world | 10–21 business days | $34.99 |

Outside the EU, prices are shown **exclusive** of local duty and tax. The
customer is the importer of record and is responsible for any charge levied on
arrival. This is the opposite of the EU arrangement in section 3 and is the
most common cause of refused deliveries.

## 5. Where Pebble does not ship

- PO boxes and poste restante addresses, in any country
- Freight forwarders and parcel-consolidation addresses
- Territories under applicable export restrictions, which the checkout blocks
  automatically
- Lithium-containing products to any address by air where the carrier declines
  the class, which in practice excludes powerbanks over 100Wh from air routes

Powerbanks and any product containing a lithium cell cannot travel by air
freight to some destinations, and will ship by surface at the times quoted for
"Rest of world" even where a faster service was paid for. The difference is
refunded automatically.

## 6. Tracking

A tracking reference is emailed within **24 hours of dispatch**. Tracking may
take a further 24 hours to show movement, which is normal and not evidence of a
problem.

Where tracking has not updated for **5 consecutive business days**, the parcel
is treated as delayed and support will open a carrier investigation. Carrier
investigations take up to 10 business days to conclude.

## 7. Lost and delayed parcels

A parcel is declared lost after:

| Destination | Declared lost after |
| --- | --- |
| United Kingdom | 10 business days past the latest quoted date |
| EU | 15 business days past the latest quoted date |
| Rest of world | 25 business days past the latest quoted date |

Once declared lost, Pebble reships free of charge or refunds in full, at the
customer's choice. Pebble does not require the customer to wait for the carrier
investigation to conclude before reshipping.

Parcels marked "delivered" by the carrier but not received are investigated
individually. Pebble requests a signature check and a safe-place photograph
from the carrier before deciding. This takes up to 7 business days.

## 8. Address changes and redirection

Addresses can be changed free of charge until the order enters picking, which
is typically within 1 hour of ordering. After that, the order must be
intercepted, which is not guaranteed and costs £5.99 where the carrier supports
it.

Pebble cannot redirect to a different country under any circumstances. The
parcel must be returned and reordered.

## 9. Delivery estimates are not guarantees

The times in this policy are targets, not contractual commitments, except for
UK Express and EU Express, where the shipping fee is refunded if the committed
date is missed. The refund covers the shipping fee only, never the order value.

Peak trading periods, specifically the two weeks before 25 December, add 1–3
business days to every service including Express. This is announced on the site
banner from 1 December.
