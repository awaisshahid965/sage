# Support Hours, Channels and Escalation

## 1. Hours

All times GMT. Pebble does not adjust published hours for British Summer Time;
during BST the local hours run one hour later.

| Day | Live chat and phone | Email |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monday to Friday | 08:00 – 20:00 | Monitored 08:00 – 20:00 |
| Saturday | 09:00 – 17:00 | Monitored 09:00 – 17:00 |
| Sunday | Closed | Not monitored |
| UK public holidays | Closed | Not monitored |

Email received outside hours is queued and the response target begins at the
next opening. The queue is not cleared overnight, so a Friday evening email is
answered Saturday morning at the earliest and Monday at the latest.

## 2. Channels and response targets

| Channel | First response target | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Live chat | 2 minutes | Order status, quick policy questions |
| Phone | 5 minutes in queue | Anything urgent, accessibility needs |
| Email | 4 business hours | Anything needing attachments or a record |
| Social media DM | 1 business day | Not for account or payment matters |
| Post | 10 business days | Formal complaints only |

Pebble does not offer support through WhatsApp, SMS, or third-party forums, and
will never contact a customer first by any of those channels. Any message
claiming to be Pebble on those channels is fraudulent and should be reported to
`security@pebble.example`.

## 3. What agents can do without escalation

- issue a refund up to £250 on an order that meets policy
- issue goodwill store credit up to £25, once per customer per 12 months
- waive standard UK shipping on a reorder
- extend a return window by up to 7 days where a Pebble error is documented
- reship a lost parcel that meets the thresholds in POL-SHP-003 section 7
- cancel an order that has not entered picking

## 4. What requires a team lead

- refunds above £250
- goodwill above £25, or a second goodwill gesture within 12 months
- any discretionary return acceptance under POL-RET-001 section 9
- waiving a restocking fee
- warranty claims rejected once and appealed
- any request to delete an account with an open order

## 5. What requires a manager or a named team

| Situation | Route to |
| --- | --- |
| Refunds above £1,000 | Commercial manager |
| Suspected fraud on an account | Risk team, `risk@pebble.example` |
| Data protection request or complaint | Data Protection Officer, see POL-PRV-007 |
| Product safety concern, including overheating or swelling batteries | Safety team immediately, and the order is flagged |
| Legal threat or regulator contact | Legal, within the same business day |
| Press or media enquiry | Communications. Agents must not comment |

A swelling or overheating battery is treated as urgent regardless of warranty
status or age. The customer is told to stop using and stop charging the device,
and Pebble arranges collection at its own cost. This overrides every other
policy in the corpus, including out-of-warranty status.

## 6. Complaint handling

1. **Stage one.** Handled by the agent. Target resolution 2 business days.
2. **Stage two.** Team lead review, requested by the customer or triggered
   automatically when stage one exceeds 5 business days. Target 5 business
   days.
3. **Stage three.** Formal written response from Customer Operations
   management. Target 10 business days. This is Pebble's final response.

After a final response the customer may refer the matter to the relevant
alternative dispute resolution scheme, and Pebble supplies the details in the
final response letter.

## 7. Accessibility

Customers who need an adjustment can request one at any point.

- extended call time with no queue pressure
- written summaries of any phone conversation, sent by email
- large-print or plain-text correspondence
- an authorised representative may act on the account with recorded consent
- callback at a specified time rather than waiting in queue

Accessibility requests are recorded on the account and applied to future
contacts automatically, so the customer does not have to ask twice.

## 8. Identity verification

Before discussing an order, an agent verifies **two** of:

- the order reference in the format `PB-nnnn`
- the email address on the account
- the delivery postcode
- the last four digits of the payment card

Agents never ask for a full card number, a password, a one-time code, or the
CVV. A caller asking a customer for any of those is not from Pebble.

Where verification fails, the agent may give general policy information but
must not confirm order details, delivery dates, or account status.

## 9. What support cannot see

Agents have no visibility of:

- carrier internal routing beyond the public tracking page
- the physical location of a parcel between scans
- another customer's order, even at the same address
- payment card numbers, which are held by the payment processor

Where a customer asks for something in this list, the honest answer is that the
information is not available to Pebble, and the agent should say so rather than
estimate.
