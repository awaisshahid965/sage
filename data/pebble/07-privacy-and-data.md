# Privacy and Data Policy

Pebble Ltd is the data controller for personal data collected through
pebble.example and the Pebble app. The Data Protection Officer is contactable
at `dpo@pebble.example`.

## 1. What is collected

| Category | Examples | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Account | Name, email, password hash | To operate the account |
| Order | Delivery address, order contents, order reference | To fulfil the contract |
| Payment | Last four digits, card brand, expiry | To identify a payment. Full numbers are never held by Pebble |
| Support | Chat transcripts, call recordings, emails | To handle and review contacts |
| Device | Serial numbers of registered products | Warranty and firmware |
| Technical | IP address, browser, device type | Security and fraud prevention |
| Marketing | Preferences, open and click data | Only with consent |

Pebble does not collect special category data and asks customers not to include
health information in support messages. Where a customer discloses a health
condition to support an accessibility request, only the adjustment is recorded,
never the underlying condition.

## 2. Retention

| Data | Kept for |
| --- | --- |
| Order and transaction records | **7 years** from the order date, as a tax obligation |
| Account details, active account | Until the account is closed |
| Account details, after closure | 30 days, then deleted |
| Support transcripts and emails | 24 months |
| Call recordings | 6 months |
| Marketing preferences and history | 24 months after last engagement |
| CCTV at Pebble sites | 30 days |
| Website analytics, aggregated | 26 months |

The 7-year retention on order records is a legal obligation and **survives an
erasure request**. A customer who asks to be forgotten has their account,
marketing data, and support history deleted, while the transaction record is
retained in restricted form until the 7 years elapse. This is the most common
misunderstanding about erasure and should be explained plainly rather than
promised around.

## 3. Legal bases

- **Contract** for order fulfilment, delivery, returns, and warranty
- **Legal obligation** for tax records and product safety
- **Legitimate interests** for fraud prevention, service security, and
  improving the store, balanced against the customer's rights
- **Consent** for marketing, and for optional analytics cookies

Consent for marketing is opt-in, never pre-ticked, and withdrawable at any time
from the account area or the footer of any marketing email. Withdrawal takes
effect within 72 hours; a message already in the send queue may still arrive.

## 4. Sharing

Pebble shares data with:

- carriers, to deliver orders, receiving name, address, and phone
- payment processors, receiving payment data directly rather than through
  Pebble
- the student and healthcare verification partner, receiving only a yes or no
- cloud hosting and email providers, as processors under contract
- authorities, where legally required

**Pebble does not sell personal data, and does not share it with data brokers,
advertisers, or affiliates for their own marketing.** Advertising on
third-party platforms uses aggregated and hashed audiences only.

## 5. International transfers

Data is stored in the United Kingdom and the European Economic Area. Some
processors operate elsewhere, and those transfers rely on adequacy decisions or
on standard contractual clauses with supplementary technical measures. A list
of processors and their locations is published at
`pebble.example/legal/processors` and updated quarterly.

## 6. Customer rights

| Right | How to use it | Time to respond |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Access a copy of your data | Account area, self-service export | Immediate, or 30 days if requested manually |
| Correct inaccurate data | Account area, or ask support | 30 days |
| Erase your data | Written request to the DPO | 30 days, subject to section 2 |
| Restrict processing | Written request to the DPO | 30 days |
| Portability | Self-service export, machine readable | Immediate |
| Object to legitimate interests | Written request to the DPO | 30 days |
| Withdraw consent | Account area or email footer | 72 hours |

Pebble may extend the 30-day period by a further 60 days for complex requests
and must tell the customer within the first 30 days if it does.

There is no charge for any of these. A repeated, clearly excessive request may
attract a reasonable fee, which has never been applied to date.

## 7. Cookies

| Type | Consent needed | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Strictly necessary | No | Session, basket, security |
| Functional | Yes | Language, recently viewed |
| Analytics | Yes | Aggregated page and journey data |
| Advertising | Yes | Audience building for third-party platforms |

Rejecting all optional cookies leaves the store fully usable, including
checkout. The consent banner offers reject-all with the same prominence as
accept-all.

## 8. Security

- transport encryption on all connections
- encryption at rest for personal and order data
- multi-factor authentication available on all accounts, and mandatory for
  staff
- staff access on a least-privilege basis, logged and reviewed quarterly
- annual third-party penetration test

Pebble will notify affected customers and the regulator within 72 hours of
becoming aware of a breach likely to result in a risk to their rights.

## 9. Children

The store is not directed at children under 16, and Pebble does not knowingly
create accounts for them. Where an account is identified as belonging to a
child, it is closed and the data deleted, except for any transaction record
held under section 2.

## 10. Automated decisions

Fraud screening is partly automated and can decline an order. This is not a
decision producing legal effects, and a customer whose order is declined may
ask for a human review by contacting support, which is carried out within 2
business days. No profiling is used for pricing; every customer sees the same
price for the same item in the same territory.
